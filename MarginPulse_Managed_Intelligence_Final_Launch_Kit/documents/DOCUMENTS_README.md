## Completion rules

Templates contain editable fields. Before client issue, complete the actual contracting provider's legal name and address, contact details, invoice/tax particulars, client entity, fees, currency, scope, retention, governing-law / dispute details and all relevant dates. Do not describe MarginPulse as a separately incorporated provider unless that is factually and legally correct.

The NDA, service agreement, SOW, invoice, privacy notice and website terms are drafts, not legal advice. Obtain local legal / accounting review for the actual provider and client jurisdictions, including cross-border data and payment issues.
